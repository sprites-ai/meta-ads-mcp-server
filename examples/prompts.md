# Example prompts for the Meta Ads MCP server

These work as written in Claude, ChatGPT, Cursor, Codex, Gemini CLI and the other clients listed in the README. Anything that changes the account comes back as an approval first.

## Read and report

- With Sprites, which Meta creatives are fatiguing and what should replace them?
- Use Sprites to compare Meta ROAS this month against last month by ad set.
- Have Sprites check which of our Meta ad sets are stuck in learning.
- With Sprites, show me what our top 3 competitors are running on Meta right now.

## Change, create and launch

- Ask Sprites for 5 new hooks for our best-performing Instagram ad.
- Use Sprites to launch a Meta campaign for our fall sale at $80 a day.
- Ask Sprites to move $50/day from the weakest prospecting ad set to the best one.
- With Sprites, pause every Meta ad with frequency over 5 and CTR under 0.5% this week.
- Have Sprites draft Reels ad concepts from our three best-selling products.

## Recurring

- Use Sprites to send me a Meta performance summary every Monday morning.

## Tips

- Name the campaign, ad set or page in full. Sprites receives only the request, not the earlier chat.
- Say the period ("last 30 days", "this quarter"); your client passes today's date so it resolves correctly.
- Working on several businesses? Say "switch to <business name>" first (`choose_business`).
- To compare channels, ask about them in one request, for example "compare Google and Meta CPA last month".
